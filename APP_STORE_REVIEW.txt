App Review notes (App Store / Google Play)
==========================================

Login is optional. Reviewers can use the app without an Apple, Google, or email account.

How to try without an account
1. Launch Pulpo / Monedero.
2. Tap “Try without an account” / “Открыть без аккаунта” / “Probar sin cuenta” on the first screen
   (or the same action on the setup screen / “Continue without signing in” on Sign in).
3. The app opens with sample local data (cash, card, salary, expenses). No cloud account is created.

Sign in with Apple
Sign in with Apple is implemented in the app. Enable the Sign In with Apple capability
for bundle id com.pulpo.app in Apple Developer and in Codemagic.
